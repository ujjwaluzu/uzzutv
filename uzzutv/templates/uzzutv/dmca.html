Copyright & removals

DMCA Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

UzzUTV respects intellectual-property rights and responds to valid copyright complaints. This policy explains how a copyright owner or authorized representative can report material associated with a UzzUTV page.

How the site works: UzzUTV does not upload, store or distribute movie, series or anime files. Our pages may show catalogue metadata and connect users to media hosted by independent third-party providers.

1. Before sending a notice

If the material is hosted by a third-party provider, the fastest way to request removal is usually to contact that provider directly. You may also send UzzUTV a notice about a page, link, embed or other material that you believe is controlled by or accessible through our service.

2. What to include

Send a clear email with the subject DMCA Notice and include all of the following:

  • Your full name, organization (if applicable), postal address, telephone number and email address.
  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed. If there are multiple works, provide a representative list.
  • The exact UzzUTV page URL, link or embed where the material appears, plus any relevant title, season or episode details.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act for the owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

3. How we review reports

We may ask for clarification or proof of authorization when a notice is incomplete or unclear. After receiving a sufficiently detailed report, we may remove or disable a page or link that we control, restrict access to associated material, contact the relevant third-party provider, or take another appropriate action.

Submitting a notice does not guarantee removal. UzzUTV may decline requests that do not identify a specific work and location, concern material outside our control, or appear fraudulent, abusive or legally insufficient.

4. Counter-notifications

If UzzUTV removes material that you believe was disabled by mistake or misidentification, you may email a DMCA Counter-Notice. Include the specific material and its former location, a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the appropriate court, your contact details, a good-faith statement that the removal was mistaken, and your physical or electronic signature.

We may forward a valid counter-notice to the original reporting party. Restoration will be considered in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances of the report.

5. Repeat infringement

Where appropriate and supported by credible reports, we may restrict accounts or access belonging to repeat infringers. Because UzzUTV does not host user-uploaded media, this provision primarily applies to misuse of accounts, links, comments or other site features.

6. Contact us

Send DMCA notices, counter-notices and copyright questions to baunthiyalujjwal52@gmail.com. Please include the relevant URL and use a clear subject line so the request can be reviewed promptly.

This policy is provided for informational purposes and is not legal advice. If you are unsure whether material infringes your rights, consult a qualified legal professional.
