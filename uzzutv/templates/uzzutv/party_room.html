How this Watch Party works

Watch together The host controls playback. Host Activity, Position Check, and guest playback stay synchronized automatically.
Video Call The host selects Video Call. The guest receives an invitation and can Accept or Decline. After connecting, use the microphone, camera, minimize, and End Call controls. On desktop, Expand opens a full-page call view: the remote video fills the screen and your self-preview stays small in the bottom-right.
Fullscreen movie Use the UzzUTV fullscreen icon in the lower-left of the player. It keeps the VidFast movie and the existing call window together. VidFast's own iframe fullscreen button cannot display UzzUTV overlays.
